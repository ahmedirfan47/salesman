# Contracts (English and Arabic)

| File | What it is | Who signs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `master-subscription-agreement.md` | The main contract: service, plans, fees, pilot, SLA, liability, term, law | Stonic and the customer |
| `data-processing-agreement.md` | How Stonic processes the customer's personal data under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) | Annex to the agreement |
| `order-form.md` | One page per customer: plan, branches, prices, start date, contacts | Both, for each deal |

Each clause is written in English, then in Arabic. **The Arabic text prevails** if the two differ, which is
what Saudi courts and arbitral bodies expect for contracts performed in the Kingdom.

## Before the first signature

1. Have a lawyer licensed in Saudi Arabia review all three files once. They are carefully drafted templates,
   not legal advice, and Stonic is not yet registered in the Kingdom.
2. Fill in the bracketed fields: `[Customer legal name]`, `[CR number]`, `[address]`, the plan and the dates.
3. Confirm the hosting regions in DPA Annex 2 against your actual Render and Vercel settings.
4. Export to Word or PDF (open the file in any Markdown editor, or ask Claude to produce the .docx) and sign.
